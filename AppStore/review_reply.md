**2. Purpose and target audience**

Basyo Memo is a to-do app that organizes tasks by the place where they happen, instead of
putting them in a single long list.

The problem it solves: in ordinary to-do apps, "restock the trash bags" and "return the
library book" sit next to each other even though they are done in completely different
places, so the list does not match how people actually remember their tasks.

In Basyo Memo, tasks live in three layers: Place → Space (room) → Task. The user chooses a
place, sees a simple floor plan, taps a room, and sees only the tasks waiting in that room.
Completing a task removes it from the room, so the space visibly becomes tidier.

Target audience: general consumers who want a calmer, more visual way to manage everyday
tasks at home, at school and at work. No specific business or organization is targeted;
the app is intended for the general public.

**3. Setup and access instructions**

No login, account, or sample file is required. The app works fully offline.

1. Launch the app. Three sample places (HOME, UNIVERSITY, WORK) and sample rooms and tasks
   are created automatically on first launch, so the app is never empty.
2. Tap a place card to open its floor plan.
3. Tap a room to see the tasks in that room. Tap a task to complete it.
4. Tap "+" at the bottom right of a room to add a task, or "+" in the navigation bar to add
   a room. Long-press a room to delete it.
5. To reach the paid feature: on the first screen, tap "New place" at the bottom. The free
   version allows three places, so the purchase screen appears. "Restore purchase" is on
   the same screen.

**4. External services used**

- RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com) — used only to manage and validate the in-app
  purchase. It receives a random identifier generated by the SDK, purchase information, and
  technical details such as device model and OS version. It is not used for advertising,
  analytics, or tracking.
- Apple StoreKit / App Store — payment processing.

No other external service is used. There is no backend server of our own, no analytics SDK,
no advertising SDK, and no AI service. All user content is stored locally with SwiftData.

**5. Regional differences**

The app's features are identical in every region. The only differences are:

- Interface language: Japanese, English and Traditional Chinese, selected automatically from
  the device language. Devices set to any other language see the English interface.
- The in-app purchase price is shown in the local currency as configured in App Store Connect.

No content, feature, or behaviour is restricted or changed by region.

**6. Regulated industry or third-party material**

The app does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no third-party protected
material. All content in the app (interface text, icons drawn from Apple's SF Symbols, and
sample tasks) is either created by the developer or provided by Apple's own frameworks.
Users only see content they create themselves.
