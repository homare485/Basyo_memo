2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE

Basyo Memo is a to-do app that organizes tasks by the place where they happen, instead of one long list. In ordinary to-do apps, "restock the trash bags" and "return the library book" sit next to each other although they happen in completely different places, so the list does not match how people remember their tasks.

Tasks live in three layers: Place, Space (room) and Task. The user chooses a place, sees a simple floor plan, taps a room, and sees only the tasks waiting in that room. Completing a task removes it from the room, so the space visibly becomes tidier.

Target audience: general consumers who want a calmer, more visual way to manage everyday tasks at home, at school and at work. No specific business or organization is targeted; the app is intended for the general public.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS

No login, account or sample file is required, and the app works fully offline.

1) Launch the app. Three sample places (HOME, UNIVERSITY, WORK) with sample rooms and tasks are created on first launch, so the app is never empty.
2) Tap a place card to open its floor plan.
3) Tap a room to see its tasks; tap a task to complete it.
4) Tap "+" at the bottom right of a room to add a task, or "+" in the navigation bar to add a room. Long-press a room to delete it.
5) Paid feature: on the first screen tap "New place". The free version allows three places, so the purchase screen appears. "Restore purchase" is on the same screen.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

- RevenueCat (revenuecat.com): used only to manage and validate the in-app purchase. It receives a random identifier generated by the SDK, purchase information and technical details such as device model and OS version. It is not used for advertising, analytics or tracking.
- Apple StoreKit / App Store: payment processing.

No other external service is used. There is no backend server of our own, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no AI service. All user content is stored locally with SwiftData.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

Features are identical in every region. The only differences are the interface language (Japanese, English and Traditional Chinese, selected automatically from the device language; any other language shows English) and the in-app purchase price shown in the local currency. No content, feature or behaviour is restricted or changed by region.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

The app does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no third-party protected material. All content (interface text, icons from Apple's SF Symbols, sample tasks) is created by the developer or provided by Apple's frameworks.
